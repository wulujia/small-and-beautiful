## 草稿

Subject: 5 written questions for a Chinese book on small, profitable products

Hi Gary,

I'm Luca — I write at wlj.me, and I spent the last decade building Zsxq (知识星球), one of China's largest paid-community platforms. I'm now writing a Chinese-language book on small & profitable products (my first book is already with a publisher) and building a public case library alongside it.

BuiltWith is a cornerstone case: 19 years, essentially one person, bootstrapped, no sales team. The internet keeps recycling the "$14M a year" story, but as Andrew pointed out back in 2015, the press rarely gets your numbers right. I'd rather print your version than the rumor — and my draft clearly labels what's unverified.

Could I email you five short questions to answer in writing, whenever convenient? No call, no recording, and you'll see the draft before anything is published.

Thanks for building something worth studying.

Luca
wlj.me

（约 148 词）

## 想问的问题

1. "$14M/年、近乎零成本"传了快十年，你从未确认或否认。愿意给一个量级或区间吗？如果不方便，至少讲讲成本结构——每周爬近 5 亿域名的基础设施，占营收的大概比例？
   EN: The "$14M a year, near-zero cost" story has circulated for nearly a decade without a word from you. Would you share a ballpark or range? If not, could you at least describe the cost structure — roughly what share of revenue goes to the infrastructure that crawls ~500M domains every week?

2. 2012 年你对 TechCrunch 说正在筹建销售团队；你和 Andrew 还定过"月入 $100k 才招工程师"的门槛——后来都没发生。哪一刻让你确定"一个人"不是阶段，而是终点？
   EN: In 2012 you told TechCrunch you were building out a sales team, and you and Andrew once set a "$100k/month before hiring a programmer" bar — neither happened. When did you decide that staying (essentially) one person wasn't a phase but the destination?

3. 早年你把 BuiltWith 挂上 Flippa 差点卖掉。19 年里想必还有过收购邀约——最接近卖掉的一次是什么时候？为什么最终留下？
   EN: You once listed BuiltWith on Flippa in the early days. Over 19 years there must have been acquisition offers — what was the closest you came to selling, and why did you keep it?

4. Wappalyzer 走浏览器插件众包路线，你坚持自建爬虫。19 年后回看，真正的护城河是历史数据、指纹库，还是别的？LLM 让爬取和识别的成本骤降，这条护城河在变宽还是变窄？
   EN: Wappalyzer went crowdsourced via browser extensions; you stuck with your own crawlers. Looking back over 19 years, what's the real moat — the historical data, the fingerprint library, or something else? Now that LLMs have collapsed the cost of crawling and detection, is that moat widening or narrowing?

5. 极端低调——不接受采访、不公布数字、不融资——是深思熟虑的策略，还是纯粹性格使然？它对这门生意是保护、加分，还是无所谓？
   EN: Your extreme low profile — no interviews, no numbers, no funding — is it a deliberate strategy or just personality? Has it protected the business, helped it, or made no difference at all?
