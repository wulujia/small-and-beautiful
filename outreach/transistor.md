## 草稿

Subject: 30 min for a Chinese book featuring Transistor?

Hi Justin,

I'm Luca, a founder based in Singapore. My day job is Zsxq (Zhishixingqiu), one of China's largest paid-community platforms for creators. I'm writing a Chinese-language book on small & profitable products — my first book is already with a publisher — and building a public case library alongside it.

Transistor is one of my anchor cases. The detail that hooked me: you and Jon projected 60 months to reach $20k MRR and got there in 11.5 — while narrating every step on Build Your SaaS. Chinese founders have almost zero exposure to the "calm company" model; I'd love to change that.

Could I get 30 minutes on a call, or send you five short questions for written answers? I'll share the draft chapter for your review before anything is published.

Luca
wlj.me

## 想问的问题

1. Profit sharing 的具体机制：拿出利润的多少比例？按什么规则分给 4 位员工？运行几年后你会改哪里？
   *How does your profit sharing actually work — what percentage of profits, split by what rule among the four employees? After running it for years, what would you change?*

2. 50/50 股权 + 对等工资，8 年里最接近"僵局"的一次分歧是什么？合伙协议里对一方退出的估值/回购是怎么约定的？
   *With a 50/50 split and equal salaries, what's the closest you and Jon ever came to a deadlock? How does your partnership agreement handle valuation and buyout if one of you wants out?*

3. Spotify 用免费托管补贴市场、YouTube 吞掉视频播客——这几年对你们新增和流失的影响，数字上到底有多大？坚持 open RSS 有没有算得出来的商业代价？
   *Spotify subsidized free hosting and YouTube is swallowing video podcasts — what did that actually do to your signups and churn, in numbers? Has the open-RSS stance had a measurable business cost?*

4. 6 个人服务 7,500+ 付费账户，招第 7 个人的触发条件是什么？这些年有人上门谈收购吗，你们怎么拒绝的？
   *Six people serving 7,500+ paying accounts — what would trigger hiring employee #7? Have acquirers come knocking, and how did you say no?*

5. 2019 年停掉公开收入，回头看 build in public 的真实成本和收益各是什么？如果重来，公开会做到哪一步为止？
   *Looking back at stopping public revenue numbers in 2019 — what were the real costs and benefits of building in public? If you started over, where would you draw the transparency line?*
