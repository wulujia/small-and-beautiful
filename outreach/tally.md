## 草稿

Subject: Tally as an anchor case in a Chinese book on small, profitable products

Hi Marie,

I'm Luca, founder of Zsxq (知识星球), one of China's largest paid-community platforms. I'm writing a Chinese-language book on small & profitable product companies — my first book is already with the publisher — and building a public case library on the founders behind them.

Tally is an anchor case: a two-person couple out-generosity-ing Typeform with unlimited free forms, now at $5M ARR with a team of eleven. One line from your April update stuck with me: "Not having a revenue goal is freeing, but also uncomfortable." I'd love to dig into that, and into the 2023 hiring detour you publicly reversed.

Could I ask for a 30-minute call, or written answers to five questions? Happy to share how Chinese makers use Tally in return.

Best,
Luca — wlj.me

## 想问的问题

1. 100 万+ 用户里 98% 不付费，免费层的托管与滥用治理成本占营收多少？有没有哪个时刻"无限免费"差点撑不住、内部认真讨论过加限制？
   （EN: With 98% of your 1M+ users on the free plan, what do hosting and abuse-prevention actually cost as a share of revenue? Was there ever a moment you seriously considered adding limits?）
2. 2023 年招 2 名工程师又反悔：用什么信号判断"开发提速不影响营收"？从发现到分手花了多久，谁先提出？
   （EN: On reversing the 2023 engineering hires — what signal told you faster shipping wasn't moving revenue, how long from realization to parting ways, and who raised it first?）
3. 你和 Filip 意见相反时谁拍板？有没有一条为了保护婚姻而定的公司规则，或者反过来？
   （EN: When you and Filip disagree, who has the final call? Is there any company rule made to protect the marriage — or the other way around?）
4. 如果 Typeform 或 Google 明天宣布无限表单免费，你们的应对预案是什么？内部推演过吗？
   （EN: If Typeform or Google announced unlimited free forms tomorrow, what's your playbook? Have you war-gamed it?）
5. 取消营收目标后，11 个人靠什么对齐优先级和绩效？NPS 从 66 到 70 的差距具体卡在哪类用户身上？
   （EN: Without a revenue goal, how do 11 people align on priorities and performance? Where exactly is the gap between NPS 66 and 70 — which users are holding it back?）
