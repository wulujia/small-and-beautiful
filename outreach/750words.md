## 想问的问题

1. 2026 年 1 月你入职 Inkitt，距离"全职冲 $50k/mo"只有 14 个月。是实验没达标，还是你本来就更喜欢"上班 + 副业"的组合？
   EN: You joined Inkitt in January 2026, just 14 months after going all-in with a $50k/mo goal. Did the experiment fall short, or do you simply prefer the day-job-plus-side-project life?

2. 2013 年给 20 万老用户终身免费——17 年后算总账，口碑收益抵得过白养他们的成本吗？重来一次还会这么大方吗？
   EN: You grandfathered 200,000 users free for life in 2013. Looking back, did the word-of-mouth outweigh the cost of serving them forever? Would you be that generous again?

3. Duolingo 靠卖 streak 修复赚钱，你从不碰。习惯机制和操纵用户之间的线，你划在哪里？有没有用户断掉几千天 streak 崩溃，让你怀疑过 streak 设计本身？
   EN: Duolingo monetizes streak repairs; you never have. Where do you draw the line between habit support and manipulation? Has anyone losing a multi-thousand-day streak ever made you doubt the streak design itself?

4. 2013 年那次投票，如果用户投的是"关站"，你真会关吗？为什么把定价的生死决定交给用户？
   EN: In the 2013 vote, if users had chosen "shut it down," would you really have? Why hand a life-or-death pricing decision to your users?

5. 2015-16 年 750 Words 已有 $12k-17k/mo，够养家，你却又打了近十年工才第一次全职。"副业长跑"是策略还是恐惧？
   EN: By 2015-16, 750 Words made $12-17k/mo — enough to live on. Yet you kept day jobs for almost another decade. Was the long side-project run a strategy, or a fear of going all-in?

6. 用户的私密文字是最敏感的数据。Fancy 档加了 AI 提示功能——你给 AI 进入这个产品定了什么红线？会允许 AI"读"用户的日记吗？
   EN: Your users' private words are the most sensitive data imaginable. With AI prompts now in the Fancy tier, what red lines have you set for AI in this product? Would you ever let AI "read" someone's entries?
