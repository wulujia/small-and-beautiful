## 想问的问题

1. 你和 Paul 六年没签股东协议，靠"对齐"运转。对齐是怎么维护的？最接近破裂是哪一次？
   You and Paul ran Fathom for six years without a shareholder agreement. How did you keep that alignment, and when did it come closest to breaking?
2. 买断在 24 小时内谈定——一家从不公开收入的公司，你们怎么定价？用了什么估值逻辑？
   The buyout was agreed within 24 hours. How do you price a company that never discloses revenue? What valuation logic did you two use?
3. "不雇人除非不雇会痛"——这条规则哪次让你付出了代价？serverless 到底替你省掉了几个人头？
   "We don't hire unless it's too painful not to" — when did that rule cost you? How many hires has serverless actually replaced?
4. 《一人公司》的理论在 Fathom 实践中哪里失灵过？Paul 心里的"enough"是一个具体数字吗？
   Where did the Company of One playbook fail in practice at Fathom? Was Paul's "enough" ever a concrete number?
5. 接手后你计划加码 enterprise sales 和付费广告——Fathom 在越过哪条线之前还算"small tech"？
   Post-buyout you planned more enterprise sales and paid ads. What line would Fathom have to cross to stop being "small tech"?
6. 看着 Plausible 靠开源在装机量上反超，如果重来，V2 还会闭源吗？
   Watching Plausible overtake you in install base via open source — knowing what you know now, would you still close-source V2?
