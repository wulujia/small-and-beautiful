**2. 2026-03-05 · Indie Hackers（James Fleischmann 文字访谈）："Hitting $25k+ MRR by making his goals less ambitious"**
https://www.indiehackers.com/post/tech/hitting-25k-mrr-by-making-his-goals-less-ambitious-jQRZDnzwm8DFAaS4Xrq0
- 核心论点（逐字）："I kept trying to start something, but failed each time. My goals were too big. I only started seeing results when I downgraded my ambition and focused on achieving small things in easy steps."——降低野心反而带来结果
- 花了两年才真正理解客户是谁："It took me two years to realize that. And it changed everything. How I market things, what I write, what I build, pricing—everything."

- 增长引擎是漏斗调优："Tuning the funnel consistently is what helped me grow my revenue."（用 PostHog 追踪 visitor→user→paying 的分源转化）
- 反鸡汤："Don't listen to any advice. Most people—myself included—don't know how to extract lessons and advice from their experiences." 建议读经济学/商业/历史/生物学的经典（"Fundamentals"）
- 下一步：自动化产品到"能离网徒步一周"，目标 $50k MRR
