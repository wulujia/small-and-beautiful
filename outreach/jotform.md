## 草稿

Subject: A Chinese book that needs Jotform's story

Dear Aytekin,

I'm Luca, a founder from China — I built Zsxq (知识星球), one of China's largest paid-membership community platforms — and I write at wlj.me.

I'm writing a Chinese-language book on small, profitable software businesses (the first book is already with the publisher) and building a public case library alongside it. Jotform anchors a chapter I can't write without you: the company that grew to 600 people and 35M users without ever raising a dime, while Typeform's funded path is the only one Chinese founders know.

Your line that "there's more to life than reaching the top of TechCrunch" — written about picking olives on your family's farm — is exactly the sensibility this book defends.

Could I have 30 minutes on a call, or send five questions for written answers? Happy to work around your schedule.

Best,
Luca (wlj.me)

## 想问的问题

1. 2005 年你辞职时 Jotform 还不存在，底气只是副业月入约 $3,000。今天你还会建议创业者走"先攒一张自己的工资单"这条路吗？AI 时代它变容易还是更难了？
   （EN: When you quit in 2005, Jotform didn't exist yet — your safety net was ~$3,000/month from side products. Would you still recommend "bootstrapping with a paycheck" today, and has AI made that path easier or harder?）
2. 20 年里你最接近拿融资的是哪一次？对方开的条件是什么，最后让你拒绝的决定性原因是什么？
   （EN: In 20 years, what was the closest you ever came to taking VC money? What was on the table, and what finally tipped you against it?）
3. 利润再投资有没有明文纪律——比如现金保留几个月、利润率低于多少就冻结招聘？还是全凭手感？
   （EN: Do you have explicit rules for reinvesting profits — months of cash to keep, a margin floor that freezes hiring — or is it judgment?）
4. "不超过 6 人的小团队"结构在 600 人、7 个办公室之后，最先在哪里失效？今天还完整成立吗？
   （EN: At 600 people across seven offices, where did the teams-of-six structure break first? Does it still hold today?）
5. 如果 Jotform 生在 2026 年——AI 产品起量快、烧钱也快、巨头跟进更快——"免费 14 个月 + 永不融资"还走得通吗？你会改哪一步？
   （EN: If Jotform were founded in 2026 — with AI products scaling and burning faster, and incumbents moving quicker — would "free for 14 months + never raise" still work? What would you change?）
