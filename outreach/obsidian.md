## 草稿

Subject: Chinese book on small, profitable products — may I feature Obsidian?

Hi Steph,

I'm Luca — I write at wlj.me and helped build Zsxq (知识星球), one of China's largest paid-community platforms.

I'm writing a Chinese-language book on small, profitable products built without VC (my first book is already with the publisher), plus a public case library alongside it. Obsidian is a chapter I can't skip: your first official non-English forum was the Chinese one, and "file over app" resonates deeply with Chinese users who've watched too many cloud note apps shut down or lock their data in.

Would you be open to a 30-minute call, or written answers to 6-8 questions? Sample: what actually changed when Sync dropped from $8 to $4, and how handing the CEO seat to a superfan really worked inside the team.

Happy to share the draft chapter for accuracy before publishing.

Luca (wlj.me)

## 想问的问题

1. 免费本地用户到 Sync/Publish 付费的转化漏斗大概什么量级？最大流失在哪一步？
   （What does the free-to-paid funnel look like — rough conversion magnitude, and where do you lose the most people?）
2. Sync 从 $8 降到 $4、商用许可改为自愿——两次"主动少收钱"内部怎么算账？实际营收影响如何？
   （You cut Sync's price in half and made the commercial license optional. How did you reason through deliberately charging less, and what actually happened to revenue?）
3. CEO 交接是谁先开的口？非创始人 CEO 的股权/激励怎么安排？"永不融资、永不卖"有没有制度化约束，还是纯靠三个人的默契？
   （Who first raised the idea of handing you the CEO seat? How is equity/incentive structured for a non-founder CEO, and is "never raising, never selling" institutionalized beyond personal trust?）
4. 拒绝 VC 之后，收购要约怎么处理？有没有报价高到内部认真讨论过的时刻？
   （How do you handle acquisition offers — has any offer ever been serious enough to trigger a real internal discussion?）
5. 中文用户占比多少？2021 年为什么把第一个官方非英语论坛给中文社区？7 个人如今怎么维护它？
   （What share of your users is Chinese? Why did the first official non-English forum go to the Chinese community in 2021, and how do 7 people sustain it today?）
6. 7 人团队怎么扛住 4,000+ 插件的安全审核和百万级用户的支持负担？哪些环节靠自动化，哪些外包？
   （With 7 full-time people, how do you handle plugin security review and support at this scale — what's automated, what's contracted out?）
