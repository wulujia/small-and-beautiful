## 草稿

Subject: One cold email, from someone writing your story into a Chinese book

Hi Nathan,

I'm Luca, writing from Singapore. I founded Zsxq (Zhishixingqiu), one of China's largest paid-community platforms for creators, and I blog at wlj.me.

I'm writing a Chinese-language book on small, profitable product companies — my first book is already with the publisher — and building a public case library alongside it. Kit is a chapter I can't skip: the Web App Challenge, the October 2014 bottom at $1,207 MRR when Hiten told you to shut it down, and the climb to $50M+ ARR without a dollar of funding.

You saved ConvertKit with cold emails to bloggers, so it felt right to reach you with one. Could I get 30 minutes on a call — or written answers to five questions? Chinese creators know Mailchimp; almost none know your story. I'd love to fix that.

Luca (wlj.me)

## 想问的问题

1. 2014 年 10 月 MRR 见底 $1,207、Hiten 劝你关掉时，从"不听劝"到"all-in"之间，真正扳动你的那个瞬间是什么？如果没等到 Pat Flynn，direct sales 你还能自费撑几个月？
   EN: In October 2014, at $1,207 MRR, what was the exact moment that flipped you from ignoring Hiten's advice to going all-in? And if Pat Flynn had never come, how many more months of direct sales could you have self-funded?

2. 你押上的不只是 $55k，还有一个已跑通的六位数个人品牌生意。回头看，关掉课程生意是期望值计算还是不认输？重来一次会更早关，还是根本不做 Web App Challenge？
   EN: You bet not just $55k but a proven six-figure ebook/course business. Looking back, was killing it a calculated bet or stubbornness? Would you kill it earlier next time — or never start the Web App Challenge at all?

3. 公开工资表 + 60% 利润分红，在 80 人、$50M ARR 规模下有没有开始变形？有没有因为透明薪酬错失过非常想要的人？
   EN: Have transparent salaries and 60% profit sharing started to strain at 80 people and $50M ARR? Have you ever lost someone you really wanted because of the public salary table?

4. 改名 Kit 和免费 1 万订阅者这两个赌注，两年后的今天你用什么指标判断赢没赢？免费计划对付费转化和 ARR 的真实账是怎样的？
   EN: Two years on, what metrics tell you whether the Kit rename and the free-up-to-10,000-subscribers plan actually won? What's the real math of the free plan's impact on paid conversion and ARR?

5. 你公开说要冲 $100M ARR，但近年增速是 14-15%/年，线性外推要六七年。你接受这个速度吗？还是 App Store / Sponsor Network 里藏着你相信的非线性引擎？
   EN: You've said publicly you're aiming for $100M ARR, but recent growth is 14-15% a year — six or seven more years on a straight line. Are you at peace with that pace, or do you believe the App Store / Sponsor Network is the non-linear engine?
