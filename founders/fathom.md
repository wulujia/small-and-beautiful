## 语录

（逐字原文 + 中译；Paul 的《一人公司》"足够"哲学优先）

### Paul Jarvis — 《Company of One》书中原句

1. **"Sometimes 'enough' or even less is all we need, since 'more' too often equates to more stress and more problems."**
   ——有时"足够"甚至更少就是我们所需的全部，因为"更多"往往等于更多压力和更多问题。（《Company of One》，via Goodreads：https://www.goodreads.com/work/quotes/59181824 ）

2. **"By removing the hurdle of having to consider scaling up in all areas at all times when things are going well, you can open yourself up to investing in enjoying your own life. You will have the freedom to enjoy the benefits of having figured out how you make enough."**
   ——移除"一顺利就必须全面扩张"这道坎，你就能把精力投到享受自己的生活上，自由地享受"想清楚怎样赚到足够"带来的好处。（同上）

3. **"Not all growth is beneficial."**
   ——并非所有增长都有益。（全书核心命题，同上）

4. **"More is generally the easiest answer, but not the smartest."**
   ——"更多"通常是最容易的答案，但不是最聪明的。（同上）

5. **"In saying no to anything that doesn't fit, you leave room to say yes to those rare opportunities that do fit."**
   ——对不合适的一切说不，你才给那些真正合适的稀有机会留出了说"是"的空间。（同上）

6. **"Your purpose is the lens through which you filter all your business decisions."**
   ——目的是你过滤所有商业决策的透镜。（同上）

### Paul Jarvis — 关于退网与退场

7. **"I don't feel like defending myself and my ideas to everyone, everywhere, all the time."**
   ——我不想无时无刻、对所有人、在所有地方为自己和自己的想法辩护。（2021-12 谈永久删除 Twitter，https://usefathom.com/above-board/quit-twitter ）

8. **"The longer I'm offline, the more trivial everything on Twitter seems."**
   ——离线越久，Twitter 上的一切就显得越无关紧要。（引自 Justin Jackson《What happened to Paul Jarvis?》，https://justinjackson.ca/paul-jarvis ）

9. **"There were definitely a few days where I thought I was definitely going to shut Fathom down for PRO customers."**
   ——确实有那么几天，我认定自己要把 Fathom 的 PRO 服务关掉了。（谈 2018 年末 Danny 退出后的至暗时刻，https://usefathom.com/blog/v2 ）

10. **"the product is always first... and Jack and my personalities are a far second."**
    ——产品永远第一……Jack 和我的个人人设远排第二。（谈创始人 IP 与产品的关系，https://usefathom.com/above-board/quit-twitter ）

### Jack Ellis

11. **"As 2024 came to a close, it became clear that Paul was ready to retire, while I felt like I had many more years left."**
    ——2024 年接近尾声时，事情变得清楚：Paul 准备退休了，而我觉得自己还有很多年可干。（买断公告，https://usefathom.com/blog/acquired ）

12. **"We actually came to an agreement within 24 hours of discussing Paul's desire for retirement."**
    ——从谈起 Paul 想退休，到达成交易共识，我们其实只用了 24 小时。（同上）

13. **"The other option for Paul was that he was going to close fathom down."**
    ——（我不加入的话）Paul 的另一个选项就是把 Fathom 关掉。（谈 2019 年初入伙，Indie Bites #35 文字稿，https://indiebites.com/35/transcript ）

14. **"It's done $150,000, which is stupid."**
    ——（Serverless Laravel 课程）已经赚了 15 万美元，离谱。（同上）

15. **"From day one, we have said your data belongs to you. We do not want to hold it hostage."**
    ——从第一天起我们就说：你的数据属于你。我们不想拿它当人质。（V4 公告，https://usefathom.com/blog/announcing-fathom-v4 ）

### 定义"一人公司"

16. **"A Company of One is simply a business that questions growth and if the growth makes sense to be a one-person business and stop there, then that's great."**
    ——一人公司就是一家质疑增长的企业；如果算下来做一个一人企业就够了、到此为止，那也很好。（Paul Jarvis 访谈原话，The Wealth Standard，https://thewealthstandard.com/the-company-of-one-mindset-rethinking-growth-in-business-with-paul-jarvis/ ）
