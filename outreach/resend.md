- 最佳渠道：Email。他的个人站 zenorocha.com 首页直接放了 Email 联系入口；Resend 团队页也公开列出他的社交账号。开发者出身，邮件是他自己的产品语言——用邮件约邮件公司的 CEO 本身就是个好开场。
- 次选渠道：X DM（@zenorocha，长期高频发帖，Resend 的增长打法就是靠他的 X 影响力）；再次选 LinkedIn（linkedin.com/in/zenorocha，他在上面也活跃发公司里程碑）。
- 触达难度评估：中低。他是"爱讲故事的创始人"——Changelog、Evil Martians、Railway、The Bootstrapped Founder 等播客都上过，讲过 110+ 场大会，明显愿意公开复盘。风险点：A 轮后事务变多，泛泛的采访邀请可能被忽略，必须用具体细节钩住；书面回答的接受概率高于视频通话。
- 个性化钩子：
  1. $10 付款链接的故事（第一个用户付的那 $10 给了他们申 YC 的信心）——细节小、少有人引用，能证明真做过功课。

## 草稿

Subject: A Chinese book on small, profitable products — can I feature Resend?

Hi Zeno,

I'm Luca, a founder in Singapore — I run Zsxq (知识星球), a paid-community platform in China, and write at wlj.me.

I'm writing a Chinese-language book on small & profitable products (my first book is already with a publisher) and building a public case library alongside it. Resend is a chapter I can't skip.

The detail that hooked me: before applying to YC, you sent your first user a $10 payment link — and he paid. That, plus React Email's docs teaching people to use it with SendGrid and Postmark, is exactly the "give first" playbook I want Chinese readers to see.

Could I get 30 minutes with you, or send five written questions instead? The one I most want to ask: how do you keep the 6-people-serving-100k-users ethos after an $18M Series A?

Thanks,
Luca
wlj.me

## 想问的问题

1. 种子轮你只拿 28 个天使的钱、把稀释压在 10%，18 个月后却接了 a16z 的 $18M A 轮——中间哪个时刻让你改了主意？邮件基础设施是不是注定无法 bootstrap？
   （EN: At seed you capped dilution at ~10% and took money only from 28 angels — then 18 months later you took a16z's $18M. What was the moment that changed your mind? Is email infrastructure simply impossible to bootstrap?）
2. 拿 VC 之后，"小而美"的哪些部分保住了，哪些被增长预期挤掉了？6 人服务 10 万用户的人效，28 人时还成立吗？
   （EN: After taking VC money, which parts of the "small and beautiful" ethos survived and which got squeezed out by growth expectations? Does the 6-people-per-100k-users efficiency still hold at 28 people?）
3. Dracula 月均 $8,000 被动收入加上书，你本可以一直过 indie hacker 的日子。all-in Resend 那一刻，你放弃的最大的东西是什么？
   （EN: With Dracula's ~$8k/month passive income plus the book, you could have stayed an indie hacker forever. When you went all-in on Resend, what was the biggest thing you gave up?）
4. React Email 文档教用户配合 SendGrid/Postmark 使用——内部有人反对过"给竞品导流"吗？从 20,000 人 waitlist 到付费客户，"give give give then ask"的真实转化率是多少？
   （EN: React Email's docs taught people to use it with SendGrid and Postmark — did anyone internally push back on "sending traffic to competitors"? And from the 20,000-person waitlist to paying customers, what was the real conversion rate of "give, give, give, then ask"?）
5. Launch HN 上最扎心的批评是"你们只是 SES wrapper"。三年后，发送基础设施自建到了什么程度？送达率上那些外人看不见的成本有多大？
   （EN: The sharpest criticism on your Launch HN was "you're just an SES wrapper." Three years on, how much of the sending infrastructure is truly yours now, and how large are the invisible costs of deliverability?）
