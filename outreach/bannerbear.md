## 想问的问题

1. 从 5 人团队回到"一个人 + AI"，中间发生了什么？主动收缩还是自然流失？
   EN: What actually happened between the five-person team and today's near-solo setup — a deliberate downsizing or natural attrition? How did you think it through?
2. 2023 年后你关掉了 open metrics——透明的成本是什么时候开始超过收益的？
   EN: You stopped publishing open metrics after 2023. When did the costs of transparency start outweighing the benefits, and what tipped it?
3. 你说用 AI 重写 V5 后发布"少了成就感"。一个人公司里，成就感没了从哪里补？
   EN: You said shipping V5 with AI felt like "less of an occasion." In a company of one, where does the sense of accomplishment come from now?
4. 12 startups 做到第 7 个 burnout。回头看，"该停"的信号是什么？那段弯路是必要的吗？
   EN: You stopped the 12-startups challenge at seven, burned out. In hindsight, what was the signal to stop — and was the detour necessary to find Bannerbear?
5. $1M ARR 之后，Bannerbear 的终局是什么？卖掉、现金牛，还是做到不能做为止？定居新加坡（对比数字游民时期）怎么影响这个答案？
   EN: Past $1M ARR, what's the endgame for Bannerbear — sell, cash cow, or run it indefinitely? And how does being settled in Singapore, versus your nomad years, change that answer?
