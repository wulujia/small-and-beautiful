## 想问的问题

1. 2021 年风波四年后复盘：那次事件真实的代价和收益各是什么？如果重来，还会用同样方式（一刀切禁令+买断）处理吗？
   EN: Four years on, what did the 2021 "Changes at Basecamp" episode actually cost you — and what did it buy? Would you handle it the same way again?

2. ONCE 的失败归因：败在 $299 定价、自托管门槛，还是"买断制"模式本身？如果再做一次，只允许改一个变量，你改哪个？
   EN: ONCE didn't work commercially. Was it the $299 price, the self-hosting friction, or the pay-once model itself? If you ran the experiment again and could change only one variable, which one?

3. 幸存者特权：如果没有 Rails 的光环和 Signal v. Noise 攒下的受众，2004 年的 Basecamp 和 2020 年的 HEY 还能冷启动成功吗？普通小团队该复制你们的哪部分、坚决不该复制哪部分？
   EN: How much of 37signals is transferable advice vs. survivor's privilege — the Rails halo, the SvN audience? What should a normal small team copy from you, and what should they not even attempt?

4. 贝索斯 2006 年那笔钱在心理层面值多少？它是否让"永不融资"这个立场，说出来比实践起来更容易？
   EN: What did the Bezos money change psychologically? Did it make "never raise money" easier to preach than it is to practice?

5. 终局：你们说 built to stay, not exit——但公司 60 人、两位创始人都年过五十。20 年后的 37signals 是什么？有接班计划，还是接受"最后一个客户关灯"？
   EN: You're "built to stay, not exit." What does 37signals look like in 20 years — succession, a sale you swore off, or someone eventually turning off the lights?
