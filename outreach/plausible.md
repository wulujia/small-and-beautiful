## 想问的问题

1. 对手是免费的：流失的试用用户里，有多大比例最终回到了"GA 免费"？"隐私付费意愿"的天花板你们怎么估——19,000 订户离顶还有多远？
   （EN: Your competitor is free. Of the trials you lose, how many go back to "GA is free"? How do you estimate the ceiling of willingness-to-pay for privacy — how far are 19,000 subscribers from the top?）
2. 首页写着 "no acquisition targets"——收到过的最大收购要约是什么量级？你和 Uku 有没有一个"什么价格都不卖"的明确约定，还是每次都重新讨论？
   （EN: Your homepage says "no acquisition targets." What's the largest acquisition offer you've received? Do you and Uku have an explicit "not at any price" pact, or do you re-debate it each time?）
3. 你在项目已运行 14 个月时以联合创始人身份加入——当时股权怎么谈的？回头看，"给营销人真正的 co-founder 股权"是不是这家公司最重要的一个决定？
   （EN: You joined as co-founder 14 months into the project — how was equity negotiated? In hindsight, was "giving the marketer real co-founder equity" the single most important decision this company made?）
4. 把 self-hosted 改名 Community Edition、降低发布频率、砍掉商业功能——社区反弹的真实成本有多大？CE 用户转化为云付费的比例有数据吗，还是它纯粹是信任装置？
   （EN: Renaming self-hosted to Community Edition, slowing releases, and cutting business features — what was the real cost in community backlash? Do you have data on CE-to-cloud conversion, or is CE purely a trust device?）
5. 四天工作制、10 个人，对面是 Matomo、Fathom 和 GA4 本身——有没有哪个具体机会是因为"保持小"而明确放弃的？AI 把内容生产成本打到零之后，你的"写作 + 社区分发"方法论还剩多少护城河？
   （EN: A 4-day week and a team of 10, up against Matomo, Fathom and GA4 itself — is there a specific opportunity you knowingly gave up to stay small? And now that AI has driven the cost of content to zero, how much moat is left in your "writing + community distribution" playbook?）
