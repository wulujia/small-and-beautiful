## 想问的问题

1. 你说"花了两年才明白客户到底是谁，从此营销、文案、产品、定价全变了"——那个洞察具体是什么？最先改变的是哪一项？
   EN: You said it took you two years to realize who your customers really are, and it "changed everything." What exactly was the realization, and what did it change first — pricing, messaging, or roadmap?

2. Reddit 早期试过没效果，2026 年你却说它是"真正跑通的渠道"。中间改变了什么？具体怎么做——哪些 subreddit、发帖还是答题、如何归因转化？
   EN: You tried Reddit early on without much result, yet later called it "the channel that really started working." What changed? What does it look like in practice — which subreddits, posting vs. answering, and how do you attribute conversions?

3. 定价从第一个 $7/月 的客户到今天 $17 起步 + 按量超额计费，中间有哪几次关键调整？涨价时怎么处理老客户？"失败不计费、缓存不计费"这类设计对留存的实际影响有多大？
   EN: From your first $7/month customer to today's $17+ usage-based plans, what were the key pricing changes along the way? How did you handle existing customers when raising prices, and how much do trust features like "failed screenshots are free" actually move retention?

4. 一个人运维 headless browser 集群四年（DO → Render → GCP → 自管 K8s），最接近崩盘的一次事故是什么？到 $33k MRR 还不招人，是财务决定、性格决定，还是对"公司"这个形态的拒绝？
   EN: Running a headless-browser fleet solo for four years (DO → Render → GCP → self-managed K8s) — what was the closest you came to a meltdown? And at $33K MRR, is staying hire-free a financial decision, a personality decision, or a rejection of "company" as a form?

5. 你 2026 年 6 月写自己是"零加速"的线性增长（+$1k MRR/月），还给出了诊断框架。现在找到那个约束变量了吗？还是说截图 API 的市场就这么大？
   EN: In June 2026 you wrote about "zero growth acceleration" — steady +$1K MRR/month — and laid out a diagnostic framework. Have you found the constraining variable yet, or is this simply the size of the screenshot-API market?

6. AI agents 现在占你新增用量和新客户的多少？对截图 API 是十年一遇的顺风，还是长期威胁（agent 最终自己驱动浏览器）？
   EN: How much of your usage and new signups now comes from AI agents? Is this a once-in-a-decade tailwind for screenshot APIs, or ultimately a threat, since agents may drive browsers themselves?

7. Product Hunt 之后你收到过收购提案。什么条件下你会卖？对你来说，一门"小而美"生意的终局是什么——$50k MRR 之后呢？
   EN: You've received at least one acquisition proposal since your Product Hunt launch. Under what conditions would you sell? What's the endgame of a small, beautiful business for you — what comes after $50K MRR?
