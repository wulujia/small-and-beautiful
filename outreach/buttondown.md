Hi Justin,

Luca (wlj.me)

## 想问的问题

1. 亲自回了七年客服邮件——如果只能归因，口碑增长中有多大比例直接来自支持体验？哪个具体时刻你决定"支持可以不再全是我"？
   In your view, what share of Buttondown's word-of-mouth growth is directly attributable to support? And what was the specific moment you decided support could stop being all you?

2. 2022 年春离开 Stripe 时 MRR 是多少？扣动扳机的是数字还是别的？"少赚两年 $750K TC"这笔账现在还会这么算吗？
   What was MRR when you left Stripe in spring 2022 — was the trigger a number or something else? Do you still frame the "~$750K TC opportunity cost" the same way today?

3. "Selling customers on what we don't have"——有没有一次因为拒绝加功能而失去大客户、让你真动摇的经历？"不做"的边界用什么标准划？
   Has refusing a feature ever cost you a big customer badly enough to shake your conviction? How do you draw the line on what Buttondown will never do?

4. 一个人维护发信基础设施，最接近崩盘的一次事故是什么？那之后你改了什么？
   What was the closest-to-catastrophe incident from running email infrastructure (deliverability, spam-flagging, KYC) essentially alone — and what changed after it?

5. Third South Capital 和 Buttondown 是什么关系——"保持小"是终点，还是通往"很多个小"的过渡？8 人之后还想变大吗？
   How does Third South Capital relate to Buttondown — is staying small the end state, or a step toward owning many small things? Past 8 people, do you want Buttondown bigger?
