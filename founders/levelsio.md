# Pieter Levels — Nomad List / PhotoAI

> 荷兰人，约 1986 年生（2013 年自述 27 岁），阿姆斯特丹读商科。solo 创业的活传奇：不融资、不雇人、不搬办公室，一台笔记本 + PHP/jQuery/SQLite + 一堆 cron job，40+ 个项目里只有 4 个成了——但这 4 个（Nomad List、Remote OK、Photo AI、Interior AI）合计月入 $20 万+。核心叙事三段式：音乐人（YouTube 频道 1 亿播放）→ 抑郁中用"12 startups in 12 months"自救（2014）→ 每一波技术浪潮（远程工作、AI 生图、vibe coding）都第一时间用最糙的方式接住。他每天发推，材料海量，本文精选策展。
> 整理日期：2026-07-16（L2 层：聚焦本人公开表达）

6. **2024-08-20 · Lex Fridman Podcast #440**："Programming, Viral AI Startups, and Digital Nomad Life"。4 小时，信息密度最高的一手材料。[视频](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oFtjKbXKqbg)｜[官方逐字稿](https://lexfridman.com/pieter-levels-transcript/)。单独展开：
   - **反主流创业公理**："I don't really raise money. I don't use VC funding, I do everything myself. I'm a designer, I'm the developer, I make everything, I make the logo. So for me, I'm much more scrappy. And because I don't have funding, I need to go fast."——两周上线一个 mini startup，用户刷信用卡才算验证。
   - **小的暴打大的**：Google 发明了 transformer、2019 年就能发 ChatGPT，却困在官僚体系里没 ship；而"being alone by myself on my laptop, in my underwear in a hotel room or something, I can ship very fast"。
   - **一切始于抑郁**：27 岁躺在公寓看天花板，月入缩到 $500，"Now I'm 27, I'm a loser"。父亲的疗法是去铲沙子——"If you're depressed, you need to get sand, get a shovel, start shoveling, doing something. You can't just sit still."——12 startups 挑战由此而来。
   - **Nomad List 的起点是一张公开 Google 表格**："It launched as a Google spreadsheet. So, it was a public Google spreadsheet anybody could edit."——众包了几百个城市的数据后才做成网站。尝试成本低到不需要写代码。
   - **技术栈保守主义**：全部产品 = vanilla HTML + jQuery + PHP + SQLite。"PHP just stays the same and works"；对新框架的态度："I'm very suspicious of money, just like lobbying. There's money in this developer framework scene."
   - **公司由机器人运转**：运维就是 `crontab -e` 里一排 PHP 脚本 + healthcheck.php 页面（绿勾/红叉表情符号）+ UptimeRobot 盯页面、出错发 Telegram。"That's how all my websites work."
   - **自由的悖论**：环球旅行 40 国后引卡夫卡——"I'm free, therefore I'm lost."无约束的自由不带来幸福，工作和目标才是意义来源。
7. **2025-07-25 · Cheeky Pint**（Stripe 联创 John Collison）："A Cheeky Pint with serial entrepreneur Pieter Levels"。[文字+视频](https://cheekypint.substack.com/p/a-cheeky-pint-with-serial-entrepreneur)｜[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=StkCdcZ1ovE)。融资 = 被迫 go big or bust，bootstrap 可以留下全部所有权；坦承数字游民生涯"extremely lonely"、拔掉文化根基要用好几年重建；解释为什么发起 EU/acc 对抗欧洲监管。

## 语录

**压低尝试成本**

1. "My challenge is to actually finish and launch my projects." — [I'm Launching 12 Startups in 12 Months](https://levels.io/12-startups-12-months)，2014-03-01
2. "🍰 Only 4 out of 70+ projects I ever did made money and grew 📉 >95% of everything I ever did failed 📈 My hit rate is only about ~5% 🚀 So...ship more" — [X](https://x.com/levelsio/status/1457315274466594817)，2021-11-07
3. "I have an idea in my mind and I build it like a mini startup, and I launch it very quickly, within two weeks or something, of building it." — Lex #440
4. "They need to take out their credit cards, pay me money, and then I can see if the idea is validated. And most ideas don't work, as you say, most fail." — Lex #440
5. "The tech stack it's not the most important of a business when you're starting out a business. The important thing is that you validate that there's a market, that there's a product that people want to pay for." — Lex #440

**不融资**

6. "I don't really raise money. I don't use VC funding, I do everything myself. I'm a designer, I'm the developer, I make everything, I make the logo. So for me, I'm much more scrappy. And because I don't have funding, I need to go fast." — Lex #440
7. "Because the odds of a venture capital (VC) funded startup are by definition stacked against you. … When you go for millions of dollars of VC investment on day one, it means you do exclude building a healthy simple business." — 《MAKE》样章，[readmake.com](https://readmake.com/)
8. "I wanted to prove you don't need to raise venture capital investment: early on I was never able to get venture capital investment, and I discovered you don't need it. Instead of raising money you can operate extremely lean and scrappy and still succeed." — [Normalization of non-deviance](https://levels.io/deviance/)，2020-12-10

**自动化**

9. "Literally, I log into the server and I do [sudo] cron tab dash E, and then I go into edit and I write hourly. And then, I write PHP, do this thing dot PHP, and that's a script, and that script does a thing and it does it then hourly. That's it. And that's how all my websites work." — Lex #440（转写文本，方括号为笔误校正）
10. "Automation helps in some respects as I can program the robots to do what I don't want to repeatedly do myself, and they can run for years, maybe decades, without reprogramming them." — [Instead of hiring people, do things yourself](https://levels.io/diy/)，2019-08-29
11. "Staring right into that void doesn't just bore me though, it scares me to death." — [How Automation Left Us Feeling Empty](https://levels.io/automation-void/)，2014-04-02（自动化尽头的空虚）

**一个人**

12. "Being alone by myself on my laptop, in my underwear in a hotel room or something, I can ship very fast and I don't need to ask legal for, 'Oh, can you vouch for this?' I can just go and ship." — Lex #440
13. "I make sure my profit margins are very high, so I try to keep the costs very low. I don't hire people." — Lex #440
14. "Apart from that I don't trust most people and I think you need a hell of a lot of money to hire people that would actually do the job better than me (arrogant yes), it also would stop me from improving my skills every day." — [Instead of hiring people, do things yourself](https://levels.io/diy/)，2019-08-29
15. "It's a big experiment to see if I can do it. The goal is to reach $1M/y as a solo founder with just automation." — [Normalization of non-deviance](https://levels.io/deviance/)，2020-12-10
