## 访谈清单

已深挖(含核心观点):

1. **2011-03-30 · Forbes《Internet Transparency: A Chat With Buster Benson》(文字访谈)** — https://www.forbes.com/sites/jasonoberholtzer/2011/03/30/internet-transparency-a-chat-with-buster-benson/
   - 公开个人数据是一种文化演化:聚合、分析、分享个人数据流会带来集体收益。
   - "The more connected you are, the more transparent you have to be in order to stay in the group."(越连接,越需要透明才能留在群体里)
   - 透明带来信任,信任带来群体红利;商业模式必须与用户利益对齐,"not trying to cheat them"。
   - 游戏层(game layer)只是动机装置,核心是对齐。

2. **2012-05-14 · TueNight《Don't Think, Act: Life According to Buster Benson》(文字访谈)** — https://tuenight.com/system-guru-q-a-with-buster-benson-part-1/
   - 有想法不列待办,直接花两小时做出来试手感:"If I have an idea, I don't do a to-do list. I just spend a couple hours building it to see if it's interesting to me or not."
   - 自我追踪的价值在"注意力"本身:"Taking the time to pay attention to something can be a calming, often enlightening experience."
   - 行为改变产品的关键是帮人记起"他本来就想做的事",而不是灌输新目标。
   - 游戏化"只需一小撮",多数人做过头:"It only takes a tiny sprinkling of gamification to get there, but most people like to go way overboard."

3. **2013-04-24 · Coach.me(Lift)博客访谈《Build Habits in 1,000 Steps》** — https://blog.coach.me/build-habits-in-1-000-steps-our-interview-with-buster/
   - 反对"微习惯":"Habits are not tiny, they're large. Building habits requires that you rewire your brain."
   - 把大目标拆成 1000 个可计数的步骤,靠大量小胜利造心理动能。
   - 训练的终点是身份改变而非行为改变:"Ultimately I am trying to train for an identity change rather than a behavior change."
   - 先改变自己,才谈得上改变世界。

4. **约 2019 年末(新书期)· Follow Your Different #118(Christopher Lochhead)《Why Are We Yelling w/ Buster Benson》** — https://lochhead.com/buster-benson/
   - 谈分歧的前提是承认自己可能没有正确答案。
   - 认知偏差源于生存机制;现代语境下"被证明错了"威胁的是社交归属,所以人会防御。
   - 把"平时视为生存威胁的人"当成长机会:"How do I treat somebody that is normally a threat to my survival, as an opportunity for growth?"
   - 沟通、读写、自省是乘数型技能,放大其他一切能力。

5. **2020-02-20 · Intercom Podcast《Buster Benson on the art of productive disagreement》** — https://www.intercom.com/blog/podcasts/buster-benson-on-the-art-of-productive-disagreement/
   - 分歧像火:"Fire can burn, and fire can heal…We need to get good at harnessing the power of disagreement."
   - 头/心/手框架:分歧分为事实之争(head)、价值之争(heart)、行动之争(hands),对症才有解。
   - 团队需要张力:"I think of disagreement as the way we sharpen each other…you need that tension."
   - 入口是观察自己的焦虑被什么点燃:"The doorway into tweaking some of these things is watching your anxiety and seeing what sparks it."

6. **约 2019-2020 · Ness Labs 文字访谈《Embracing the discomfort of self-reflection》(Anne-Laure Le Cunff)** — https://nesslabs.com/buster-benson-interview
   - "Writing, to me, is scaffolded thinking."(写作是搭了脚手架的思考)
   - 日记的本质是"反复重新进入自省、自我怀疑、自我转化的不适"。
   - 私密写作与公开发表是相反的肌肉;750 Words 刻意做成"所有发布平台的反面"。
   - 写作习惯养成后"像一个可以回去的家"。

7. **2022-04-21 · The Knowledge with David Elikwu《Follow Your Curiosity with Buster Benson》** — https://theknowledge.io/buster/
   - 人生当游戏玩,规则是用来 hack 的;上学时就追求"用最小努力拿 A-"。
   - 刻意做通才:"I wanted to be a level one everything…a level one designer, level one software developer."
   - 副业是学习实验室,"nine out of ten of them don't (succeed)",但每个都教你一些东西。
   - 做自己回报更高:"There is a lot of reward to being yourself…than there is from hiding it and forcing people to try to guess who you are."

8. **2024-05-09 · The Knowledge with David Elikwu《Curiosity, Creativity, and Careers》** — https://theknowledge.io/busterbenson-1/
   - 27 岁时故意拆掉自己的人生,用一千天做行为改变实验,回答"How do we change ourselves?"
   - 职业导航靠真问题(对人类行为与技术的好奇),不靠预设路径。
   - 技术的道德两面性:互联网既普惠发声,也放大操纵与错误信息。
   - 后期工作重心从"how do I profit?"转向"how do I contribute without causing harm?"。
